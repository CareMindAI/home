1 · The problem

Missing medicine is common - and nobody notices.

সমস্যাটা কোথায়?

Here are the facts, in simple numbers.

1 in 2

long-term patients do not take medicine as prescribed. It is even worse in developing countries.

Source: WHO

1.53 crore

Bangladeshis are aged 65 or older - the people most likely to take many medicines.

Source: Census 2022, via TBS

Handwritten

prescriptions are still normal in Bangladesh, and hard for patients to read.

Source: IUB, PresCa study

The good news

The phone to fix it is already in the house.

89.6% of people use a mobile phone

75.1% of households have a smartphone

58.2% have home internet - so we also plan SMS and phone-call reminders

Source: BBS via UNB · Elders are going digital: Dhaka Tribune, SAMENA

One small mistake at home can grow into a big cost.

  1. ConfusionCan't read the prescription
  2. Wrong dosePill taken twice, or not at all
  3. Gets sickerSugar, pressure or infection out of control
  4. HospitalExtra visits, tests and costs
  5. Family paysMoney, missed work, worry

CareMind AI stops the problem at the first two steps - before anything goes wrong.

2 · How it works

From paper prescription to reminders in four easy steps.

কীভাবে কাজ করে?

No typing. No reading the doctor's handwriting.

  1. 1Take a photo

    Snap the prescription with the camera, or pick it from the gallery.

    Working
  2. 2AI reads it

    Medicine names, times (like 1-0-1), before/after food and days appear. You check and fix.

    Working
  3. 3Get reminded

    The phone rings at each dose. Tap Taken or Missed.

    Working
  4. 4Family knows

    Children and relatives see every dose and get an alert if one is missed.

    Coming soon

5 · The difference

Rahima's day, before and after.

রহিমার দিন - আগে ও পরে

Rahima is an example of a typical elderly patient taking 5 medicines a day.

Today

  • Guesses what the doctor wrote
  • Remembers 5 medicines a day by memory
  • Takes some doses twice, misses others
  • Family hears "Yes, I took it" and just hopes
  • Problems are found only at the hospital

With CareMind AI

  • One photo turns the prescription into a clear plan
  • The phone reminds her at every dose, in Bangla
  • Each dose is marked - no doubling, no guessing
  • Family sees the truth and gets an alert if she misses one
  • Problems are caught early, at home

Safe and trustworthy নিরাপত্তা ও বিশ্বাস

What if there is no internet?

SMS and phone-call reminders. A son or daughter can set it up and check from far away.

What if the AI reads it wrong?

You check and confirm every detail before saving (already built). A pharmacist check is planned.

Is my health data private?

Each family member must give permission. We keep minimal data, encrypt it, and you can remove access anytime.
