The problem in numbers

A common, hidden problem — and the phone to fix it is already in the house.

Elderly patients guess, double or miss doses from handwritten prescriptions, and their families find out too late.

What one wrong dose can lead to

Small mistakes at home grow into big costs for the family.

  1. ConfusionUnreadable handwriting, many medicines, many timingsWe fix this
  2. Wrong or missed doseA pill taken twice, a pill not taken at alland this
  3. Health gets worseBlood pressure, sugar or infection is not controlled
  4. Doctor or hospitalExtra visits, tests and medicine costs
  5. Family paysMoney, missed work and constant worry

Our platform breaks the chain at the first two links, before anything goes wrong.

How it works

From paper prescription to daily reminders in four steps.

  1. 1Scan

    Take one photo of the handwritten prescription — camera or gallery.

  2. 2AI reads, you confirm

    Medicines, 1-0-1 pattern, food timing and days appear. Check and edit.

  3. 3Remind

    The phone rings at each dose. Tap taken or missed.

  4. 4Family knows Planned

    Relatives see taken and missed doses and get alerts.

No typing. No reading the doctor's handwriting. Steps 1–3 already work in our prototype.

Global competitors

  • Medisafe: family alerts work; 71% of non-adherent users improved (company study).
  • MedSnap, GoodPill, RxScan: read printed labels only, not handwritten Bangladeshi prescriptions.
  • memorAIs: research shows the scan-to-reminder approach works.

Risks and mitigation

Elders with weak internet

SMS and voice-call reminders; a relative can set up and monitor.

AI misreads a dose

User confirms every field (already built); optional pharmacist check.

Health data privacy

Consent per family member, minimal data, encryption, revoke anytime.
